# Archived is a drawer under Settled, the chat list resizes from its edge, and Ctrl plus and minus zoom the app

> **Accepted.** It changes ADR 0090's order of the list's scroll: Archived leaves the scroll and becomes a second drawer at the list's foot, under Settled. It also changes ADR 0063's default: Archived now shows until the owner turns it off. The order is `SECTION_KEYS` in `src/hermes/sectionKeys.ts`, and the drawers are `DRAWERS` in `src/ui/Sidebar.tsx`. The edge is `ResizeHandle` in `src/ui/ResizeHandle.tsx`, and the zoom is `followZoomKeys` in `src/ui/zoom.ts`. Tested in `src/ui/chatSections.test.tsx`, `src/ui/chatListResize.test.tsx` and `src/ui/zoom.test.ts`.

The owner asked for three changes to the main window. The Archived section sat in the list's scroll, under a long Inbox, and they wanted it at the bottom, under Settled, with a way to hide it. The chat list had a fixed width of 272 pixels, so long titles were cut short and a narrow window lost room to it. They wanted to drag the list narrower or wider, without the drag ever closing it. They also wanted Ctrl plus and Ctrl minus to zoom the app, as a browser does.

## Decisions

- **Archived is a drawer at the list's foot, under Settled.** It works as the Settled drawer does: its header always shows, with the count, and opens the drawer upward with its own scroll. A closed drawer draws no rows. An open one draws ten rows, then Show more (ADR 0185). The two drawers share at most half of the panel. Up and Down move from the scroll into Settled, and from Settled into Archived. A row dragged onto Archived does nothing, because archiving is a write and a drag stays a settle or an unsettle (ADR 0090).
- **Archived shows until the owner turns it off.** The switch is the one Settings → General → Chat list sections already has, so the Settings list is in the list's order: Pinned, Inbox, Snoozed, Working, Settled, Archived. A stored list of shown sections is kept as it is. An owner who never turned Archived on keeps it hidden. There is no migration, because a stored list can't tell a choice from an old default. Hiding or showing Archived changes only whether the app lists archived chats (`listArchived`). It never archives, restores or deletes a chat.
- **The list's right edge resizes it.** Drag the edge, or focus it and press the arrow keys, Home or End. The list follows the pointer continuously. It stops at 200 pixels, where New chat, Search and a title's start still show, and at 560 pixels. It also stops where the chat would have less than its 440 pixel minimum beside it. A drag never hides the list. Ctrl+B and the rail button still hide and show it, and nothing else does. The width is kept in the display preferences (`sidebarWidth`) when the drag ends. A smaller window, or a zoom, narrows the list for as long as it lasts, and the kept width comes back once there is room.
- **The list never squeezes its own rows out.** At least two rows of its scroll stay in view, and each drawer keeps its header however full the other one is. A window too short for all of it, small or zoomed in, scrolls the list's contents whole, so the Inbox, both drawers and the controls above them stay reachable.
- **One resize handle for both panels.** The side panel's left edge and the chat list's right edge use `ResizeHandle`. It ends a drag when the pointer is let go anywhere, when the drag is cancelled, when it loses the pointer, or when its panel goes mid-drag. Each of these keeps the last width and clears the resize cursor.
- **Ctrl+= or Ctrl++ zooms in, Ctrl+- zooms out, and Ctrl+0 resets.** Ctrl+Shift+= and the keypad's plus also zoom in. The steps are a browser's, from 50% to 200%. The commands are in the keybindings table, so Settings → Keyboard Shortcuts lists them and the owner can change them. The keys work anywhere a key reaches the window, which includes Sign in, Setup and Settings. A control that takes a key first keeps it: the Terminal keeps every key. The search palette and Settings' narrow navigation sheet, which keep the app's other shortcuts from reaching past them, let the zoom keys through.
- **The zoom is the webview's own.** `Webview.setZoom` sets WebKit's or WebView2's zoom level, so the whole page lays out at the new size. The web page view's placement already converts the app's CSS pixels to GTK's by the viewport's width (ADR 0081), so it stays over its pane. Tauri's own zoom keys stay off (`zoomHotkeysEnabled` is false by default), so one press never zooms twice. The capability adds only `core:webview:allow-set-webview-zoom`. The zoom is kept in the display preferences (`zoom`) and set again at launch.

## Considered options

- **Archived as the last section of the scroll, after Working.** Rejected. With a long Inbox, the owner would still scroll to find it, which is what they asked to stop.
- **Turning Archived on for everyone with a migration.** Rejected. A stored list without Archived can be an owner's choice, and a migration would undo it.
- **A CSS `zoom` or `transform` on the page's root.** Rejected. It changes the CSS pixel size only for the app's own boxes, so the measurements the web page view, pointer positions and the screenshot tools take would no longer match the screen.
- **Tauri's `zoomHotkeysEnabled`.** Rejected. On Linux and macOS it injects a script with fixed 20% steps that isn't kept across a restart. It also catches the keys before the app's handlers, so it can't follow the owner's keybindings or leave the Terminal alone.
- **A drag below the minimum that hides the list.** Rejected. The owner asked for the drag never to close it, and Ctrl+B is the way to hide the list.

## Consequences

- The list fetches the profile's archived chats on each refresh by default, as ADR 0063 says Archived does while shown. An owner with many archived chats can turn the section off in Settings.
- Tests and the feature map find the Archived drawer by `.archived-drawer` and its rows by `.archived-list`, the Settled drawer by `.settled-drawer`, and both drawers in `.sb-foot`, the scroll's next sibling.
- Zoomed far in a small window, the chat can get less than its 440 pixel minimum: at 200% the 760 pixel minimum window is 380 CSS pixels wide, and the rail and the 200 pixel list leave the chat about 124. The list's minimum holds, so its controls stay whole, and Ctrl+B hides the list to give the chat the room.
- The width and zoom are kept for this computer only, like the other display preferences, and are included in a settings backup.
